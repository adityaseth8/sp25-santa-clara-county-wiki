===== Recall =====
A metric showing how good a system is at finding all the correct cases. It tells us how many of the actual positive cases were successfully caught by the system. Recall is also known as the true positive rate.

=== How it is Used ===
Suppose the County uses a system to find fraudulent social services claims. If there are 10 real fraudulent claims, and the system only catches 4 of them, then the recall is 4 out of 10, or 40%. A high recall means the system is good at finding most of the actual cases it should catch.
