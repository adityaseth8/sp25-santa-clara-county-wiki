===== Patch =====

A small software update that fixes bugs (functionality issues), security issues, or adds new features.

=== How it is Used ===
The County may send out patches as quick fixes to public-facing tools and services, like the County’s Public Permit Portal. Patches are often temporary solutions meant to keep things running smoothly until a more complete update can be made later.
