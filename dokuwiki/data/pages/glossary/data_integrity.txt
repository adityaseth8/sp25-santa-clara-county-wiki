===== Data Integrity =====

Data integrity means making sure that data is accurate and complete at any point during its lifecycle. It also ensures data is consistent, so that it stays the same and up to date across different systems. Data integrity ensures the reliability of data, which allows people to trust the data being used for reports and important decision making.


=== How it is Used===
At the County, data integrity promotes strong service delivery and decision making. For example, systems may have automatic checks to prevent users from submitting incomplete forms. County departments may also conduct regular audits to proactively identify and fix data issues.
