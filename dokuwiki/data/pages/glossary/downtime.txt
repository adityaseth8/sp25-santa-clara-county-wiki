===== Downtime =====

Period of time where a system is unavailable for its intended use due to system failures, ongoing software updates, or other unexpected issues.

=== How it is Used ===
Sometimes, downtime is planned, such as during the scheduled maintenance of a web application. The County's IT Strategic Plan FY24-26 outlines a goal of having 99.95% uptime of infrastructure to ensure County services remain accessible.
