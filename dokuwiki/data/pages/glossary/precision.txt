===== Precision =====

A metric showing how often a model's predictions of something being positive (or true) is actually correct.

=== How it is Used ===
Suppose the County uses a system to detect fraudulent claims in a social services program. If the system flags 10 claims as fraudulent, but only 1 is actually fraudulent, then the precision is 1/10 or 10%. Typically models strive for high precision, which means fewer false positives are predicted by the model (fewer claims that were wrongly flagged as fraudulent).
