===== Scalability =====

The ability to increase or decrease the computational resources required to execute a varying volume of tasks, processes, or services. Scalability allows systems to tackle more complex or larger scale problems effectively.<sup>1</sup> This helps keep services fast and reliable, even when demand changes.

=== How it is used ===
A key application of scalability is in web applications. The County can scale up or down computing power for web applications based on the demand. Scalability is a key factor in capacity planning, ensuring that resources are available when needed while optimizing the cost of operation.
