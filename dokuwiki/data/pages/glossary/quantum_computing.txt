===== Quantum Computing =====

== Overview ==
Quantum computing leverages quantum-mechanical principles to process information in ways that classical computers cannot. Quantum bits, or qubits, enable superpositions and entanglements, allowing quantum computers to solve specific types of problems much faster than conventional computers.<sup>1</sup> Quantum computing has the potential to revolutionize areas such as cybersecurity, financial modeling, and optimization problems.
