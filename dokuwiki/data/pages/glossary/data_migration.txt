===== Data Migration =====

Permanently moving data from one storage location, system, or format to another. This process often follows best practices set by federal agencies like NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology). Some key aspects of data migration involve keeping the data secure, ensuring data accuracy, minimizing downtime, creating clear documentation, and testing to ensure everything works after the migration. 

=== How it is Used ===
Data Migrations are used in the County to modernize systems and improve efficiency of service. For example, the Office of the Assessor recently completed the **Migration of Web and Database Servers to Cloud**.
