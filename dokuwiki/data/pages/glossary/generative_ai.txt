===== Generative Artificial Intelligence (Gen AI) =====
Type of AI which produces new content in the form of either text, images, videos, audio, or code. Typically Gen AI systems generate something new by using patterns it's learned from past data it's been trained.

=== How it is Used ===
Examples of Gen AI systems include large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, which can generate text to answer questions, write emails, code, and more. It can also be used to summarize long documents so that they are easier to understand. However, it's vital to use Gen AI systems carefully, as they can sometimes provide incorrect or misleading output. They can also reflect biases found in its training data.

=== FAQ: Algorithm vs. Generative AI ===
Algorithms will generally produce consistent outputs whereas Gen AI will generally produce varied outputs.
