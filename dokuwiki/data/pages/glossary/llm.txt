===== Large Language Models (LLMs) =====
Type of AI that can understand and create human-like text. It is trained on massive amounts of text data—like books, websites, and articles so that it can recognize patterns in how people write and talk.

=== How it is Used ==
LLMs can be classed into two types: generative or discriminatory. Generative LLMs are models that output text, such as the answer to a question or even writing an essay on a specific topic. Discriminatory LLMs are supervised learning models that usually focus on classifying text, such as determining whether a text was made by a human or AI.<sup>1</sup>

Tools like ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot are examples of LLMs.
