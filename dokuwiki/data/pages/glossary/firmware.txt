===== Firmware =====

Computer programs and data stored in hardware - typically in read-only memory (ROM) or programmable read-only memory (PROM) - such that the programs and data cannot be dynamically written or modified during execution of the programs.<sup>1</sup>

=== How it is Used ===
Firmware is used in many devices. For example, traffic signal controllers rely on firmware to manage the timing and sequencing of red, yellow, and green lights.
